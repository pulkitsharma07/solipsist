Why small teams ship faster

Every team I have worked on believed it needed more people. 1Adding engineers to a late project makes it later, and yet we keep hiring because headcount is the one lever a manager can pull without being wrong in public.

The alternative is duller than it sounds. You cut scope until the thing fits in one head, and you let that head decide. Nobody enjoys this part, which is most of why it works.

By the time the second quarter arrives, 2the small team has already shipped twice and learned what the big team is still arguing about. That gap compounds.

None of this is new. It is just rarely done, because it feels like doing less.
