**Page review** (in the [agent panel](/docs/reference/agent-panel/) and the
[status bar](/docs/reference/status-bar/#page-review)) sends the whole page to an agent that can only leave comments.
It can't change the text. Its questions show up in the margin, in blue, each next to the passage it's about.

<Shot name="review-margin" title="Review notes in the margin" alt="Two review questions as short notes in the margin" width={300} />

Open one to read the whole question and the passage it quotes. From there you can **Ask** the reviewer about it, or
**Dismiss** it, and it won't come up on that passage again.

<Shot name="review-open" title="Review question" alt="A review question opened under its passage, with Dismiss and Ask" />

The reviewer's questions are scored and filtered before you see them, so you get a few useful ones instead of one on
every line. The `review.*` settings control how many are kept overall and per block (see
[configuration](/docs/reference/configuration/)), and the `review` prompts control what the reviewer looks for (see
[Prompts](/docs/agents/prompts/)).
